END-TO-END INTELLIGENCE

From stockroom surplus to community impact.

A unified digital platform replaces fragmented spreadsheets, late-stage expiry dumping, and unauthorized intermediaries with algorithmic trust.

STEP 01

Batch Intake

Capture batch numbers, exact quantities, storage temperatures (ambient, cold-chain), and donor credentials upon receipt.

STEP 02

Expiry Risk Triaging

Automated classification tags inventory as Safe (>90d), Monitor (>60d), Attention (>30d), or Critical (<30d).

STEP 03

AI Surplus Forecasting

Predicts localized demand velocity against remaining shelf-life, identifying surplus weeks before potential expiry.

STEP 04

Proximity Matching

Instantly computes matching scores (urgency, travel distance km, license verification) to find eligible beneficiary NGOs.

STEP 05

Traceable Transfers

Monitors consignment progress through 6 auditable stages with digital waybills and QR batch verification.

STEP 06

Certified Eco-Disposal

Routes unviable stock directly to CPCB-authorized biomedical waste incinerators with cryptographic destruction manifests.
